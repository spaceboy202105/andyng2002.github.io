\section{Research and projects}
\entry{Towards Valid B-Rep Generation: Training-Free Wireframe Anomaly Detection and Repair}{First author; submitted to CVM, decision pending}{Dec 2025 - May 2026}
Preprint: \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04955}{arXiv:2608.04955}
\begin{itemize}
\item Proposed training-free WDR, combining GTAD anomaly detection and EGGTR energy-guided repair at the wireframe stage. Detects and routes self-intersections, collapsed edges and disconnected vertices for local regeneration; supports autoregressive and diffusion B-Rep generators without retraining the base model.
\item Integrated WDR into DTGBrepGen, Stitch-A-Shape and BrepForge. On 3,000 paired samples, improved DTGBrepGen validity from 79.5\% to 95.6\% on DeepCAD and from 68.1\% to 83.9\% on ABC, largely preserving generation diversity and distribution quality.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Stack:} Python, PyTorch, Diffuser, PythonOCCT

\entry{TrussOrbit: Edge-Orbit Sequence Modeling for Truss Metamaterial Inverse Design}{First author; submitted to ICLR 2027, decision pending}{Jul 2026 - Sep 2026}
\begin{itemize}
\item Proposed EOTR to jointly encode node geometry and connectivity through edge-orbit sequences. Compact sequences and symmetry expansion reconstruct full trusses for target-stiffness-driven autoregressive inverse design.
\item Proposed set-level reinforcement learning with SMCO: finite-element feedback and empty-slot counterfactual evaluation assign sequence-level marginal credit, combined with GSPO to jointly optimize stiffness matching and physical validity.
\item On a unified benchmark of 1.49 million structures built by our team, outperformed all compared baselines in speed and reconstruction error for single-shot inverse generation from stiffness matrices, while supporting a larger topology design space.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Stack:} Python, PyTorch, VeRL, SGLang
